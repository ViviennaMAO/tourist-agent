# Las Vegas — first reference-led visual pass

The supplied daytime Strip, evening fountain, Sphere screen and Welcome Sign photographs guide this pass. It is a daytime miniature scene; the sunset/night lighting and Paris Eiffel Tower remain later work.

## Implemented

- 31 existing ordinary buildings now have pale limestone/plaster hotel façades, dark blue/bronze glazing, reveals, sills, pilasters, restrained cornices, rooftop volumes and retail canopies. Original footprints and collisions remain in place.
- A new lakefront hotel and elliptical fountain basin occupy a reserved southern parcel. The reserve participates in procedural placement before rocks and buildings scatter, while only the active hotel/lake/trees add walking obstacles. Existing landmarks, the boulevard and welcome-sign approach remain clear.
- The lake has a pale stone promenade/rim, moving fine-wave normals, local reflected hotel façades and 36 choreographed water jets. Continuous translucent water cores, travelling droplets and a ripple band share the same timing. This is an authored fountain sequence, not a recreation of a specific Bellagio show.
- Sphere's coarse emoji shell is replaced in the enhanced view by a smoother cut sphere, podium glazing and an animated red/white/blue star-and-band programme. Filtered LED detail and view-dependent edge brightness preserve its rounded form. Source visibility and navigation are restored on toggle/disposal; the screen is not a live feed or the supplied photograph.
- Added nine branching lakefront shade trees; upgraded 29 existing palms, 18 traffic vehicles, street crossings/drains and mainland shoreline materials with the established reusable city modules.
- Welcome Sign keeps its recognisable source artwork and gains a paved approach and refined material response. Other source landmark textures receive restrained material/colour adjustment. Desert rock colour and surface roughness are softened, while their original coarse silhouettes remain.
- Added two static local reflection probes, nearby contact shadows and review viewpoints. Other cities retain their existing geometry and lighting settings.

## Verification

- Syntax checks, 120 tests and production build. New tests cover lake reservation/coverage, bounded fountain animation, reversible collision ownership, delayed Sphere loading, source preservation and ordinary-building instance restoration.
- Actual browser review covers five Vegas views, lake-edge walking/water exclusion, all six Vegas checkins, Before/After restoration, Tokyo and Abu Dhabi views, and return to Dubai. All 25 assets must load and console/runtime error lists must remain empty.
- The initial visual review showed faint fountain particles; continuous translucent jet segments were added. The lake obstacle test also exposed a seam at exact strip boundaries, corrected with a small overlap.
- Reflections are local cube-map approximations; the ocean retains the existing planar reflection pass. Mobile performance has not been benchmarked. The existing large Three.js bundle advisory remains.

## Remaining work

Paris/Eiffel architecture, a broader irregular Bellagio-style waterfront and fully choreographed night lighting are not yet implemented. Generic buildings still repeat window rhythms; the original Luxor/Resorts World/Stratosphere shells and desert mesas remain simplified. Lake water does not model fluid dynamics, and the city is an island composition rather than a geographic reconstruction of the Strip. These are the next separate refinement targets, not completed details.
