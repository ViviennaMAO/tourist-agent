Turn city discovery
into a real-world visit.

A browser-based destination experience connecting immersive city scenes, practical itineraries and participating local businesses.

城市旅行护照：让游客先发现迪拜，再把喜欢的地方变成真实旅行。

Explore a scene → Discover a place → Save an itinerary → Visit official services

What visitors can try today

  • Dubai and Abu Dhabi city themes, with five explorable interior studies per city.
  • Eva’s short guided observations, with a direct route to place information.
  • A Dubai Marina half-day itinerary: Marina Mall, the adjacent waterfront and Pier 7.
  • Saved itineraries, text export, real map links and official destination information.

Proposed first pilot

  • One compact Dubai district, selected with local operators.
  • 10–20 participating merchants before consumer promotion; invited visitors first.
  • A comparison of a short 3D entry with a direct itinerary entry.
  • Abu Dhabi content preparation in parallel; commercial expansion only after the first pilot meets its gates.

What we would like to explore with DET

A content and visitor-experience contact, introductions to destination operators and a suitable merchant cluster, approved content and brand-use guidance, and a limited distribution opportunity after readiness review.

Two separate measures of success

Destination experienceParallel World services
Useful itinerary actions; verified bookings and visits where operator data is available; improvement against a comparison group.Verified net spending, merchant outcomes and reliable payment, refund and settlement flows once integrations are operational.

Destination content and pilot operations can be scoped as a service engagement. Merchant payment services and economic rules require separate integration and agreements.

Current readiness: This is an independent experience prototype. Interiors are authored thematic miniatures, not surveyed or officially endorsed reproductions. No tourism authority endorsement, merchant partnership, ticket sale, verified visit or live payment is implied. Prototype event records are local to one browser and are not aggregate visitor or transaction data.
